08Objection Handling + Search Intent

Forensic engineering FAQs.

What does a forensic engineer investigate?
A forensic engineer investigates the cause, extent, and contributing factors of property damage, construction defects, structural distress, material or component failures, and other engineering-related conditions. The investigation may include site observations, measurements, documentation, testing, record review, and engineering analysis to determine what the physical evidence supports.
When should a forensic engineer be retained?
A forensic engineer should be considered when the cause or extent of damage, a defect, or a failure is unclear, disputed, or may affect a claim, repair decision, legal matter, or other significant determination. Early involvement can also help identify and preserve relevant physical evidence and documentation before conditions change or repairs are made.
What types of property damage does TCE investigate?
TCE investigates a range of property damage and building conditions, including storm and wind damage, hail damage, roof damage, water intrusion, building envelope failures, structural distress, and other conditions requiring engineering analysis. Each investigation is tailored to the property, reported event, observed conditions, and questions that need to be addressed.
Does TCE investigate construction defects?
Yes. TCE investigates suspected construction and design defects, installation deficiencies, building envelope conditions, structural concerns, material or component failures, and other construction-related issues. Engineers evaluate the available physical evidence and relevant documentation to identify potential causes and contributing factors.
Can TCE determine the cause of roof or building envelope damage?
TCE performs forensic investigations of roofing systems, water intrusion, exterior walls, building envelope components, and related damage or performance issues. Depending on the matter, engineers may evaluate storm-related damage, installation or construction conditions, deterioration, material performance, maintenance, and other potential contributing factors to develop engineering findings supported by the available evidence.
Does TCE provide expert witness and litigation support services?
Yes. TCE provides forensic engineering, litigation support, technical analysis, expert reports, and expert witness services for matters requiring engineering expertise. TCE works with attorneys and other stakeholders to investigate technical issues, evaluate evidence, communicate engineering findings, and provide expert testimony when appropriate.
What should I have available before contacting a forensic engineer?
You do not need to have everything assembled before contacting TCE. Helpful information may include the property or project location, a brief description of the issue, when the damage or condition was discovered, photographs or videos, plans or drawings, inspection or repair records, claim-related documents, and other relevant reports or correspondence. TCE can help identify what additional information may be useful after learning more about the matter.
