02 Common Questions
Do you work outside of Florida?↓

Yes. TCE is licensed in 30 states, the District of Columbia, and Puerto Rico — 32 jurisdictions in all — and our forensic practice regularly takes us across the country.

How fast can you turn around a proposal?↓

For most projects and claims, you’ll have a written scope and fee within 1–2 days of our first conversation. Rush assignments happen all the time in this business; say so and we’ll give you a realistic timeline that we can meet.

Can you handle the engineering of a whole building, not just structure?↓

That’s the point of the firm. Structural, mechanical, electrical, and plumbing design under one roof, from schematic design through construction, so you’re not coordinating three consultants to get one building done.

Will your forensic report hold up in litigation?↓

Yes! Our evaluations document cause & origin of damage and scope of repair with the detail that insurance carriers, opposing experts, and courts expect. Every report is prepared to be defensible, because many of them end up needing to be.

Full-service engineering: structural, MEP, forensics, and product R+D — from schematic design through construction.
