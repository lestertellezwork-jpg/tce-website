Common Questions

Answers, up front.

What is CFS delegated design?
On many cold-formed steel projects, the fabricator must provide engineered, sealed calculations and drawings for their panels or trusses. TCE provides that delegated engineering — coordinated with the project architect and engineer-of-record, sealed in the project's state.
What panelized systems does TCE engineer?
TCE produces per-panel shop files, opening schedules and shear-wall packages for machine-fabricated systems, including FRAMECAD panelized communities where TCE serves as engineer-of-record.
How fast is TCE's CFS turnaround?
Fast enough to protect the construction schedule — that is the practice's core promise. Scoped proposal in days; delivery dates we commit to and hit.
