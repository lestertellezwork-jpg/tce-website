Hidden Creek
cabin community.

Ground-up structural design for a multi-cabin development — from helical pile foundations to roof truss layouts.

The assignment

Hidden Creek is a multi-cabin community designed for buildability from the ground up — literally. TCE engineered the full structural package across four cabin designs: helical pile foundations with engineered girder-to-pile connection details, pile schedules coordinated to manufacturer specifications, and floor and roof truss layouts for each cabin type.

Design information packages, structural plans, and calculation sets moved together so the field never waited on the engineering — the same design-it-to-build philosophy we bring to cold-formed steel and every other structure we touch.
