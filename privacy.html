Privacy

Your information,
handled like our reports.

Documented, minimal, and used only for the purpose you gave it to us. Here’s the plain-language version.

Effective August 2026 · Updated November 2026

What we collect

When you submit a form on this site — a proposal request, a call booking, a resource request, or a job application — we collect what you type: your name, email, phone number, the state and nature of your project or claim, and any message you include. We also record which page brought you to us so we know how you found TCE. We also use analytics and advertising measurement technologies, described in the “Website Analytics, Digital Advertising & Cookies” section below.

How it’s used

Form submissions go to Trillas Consulting Engineers by email and are used for one purpose: responding to you and, if we work together, running the engagement. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for their marketing. If you request a resource or opt into updates, every email includes a working unsubscribe.

Cookies & local storage

This site uses your browser’s local storage for small conveniences — remembering your motion-and-effects preference, dismissed notices, and your cookie-consent choice. Analytics and advertising cookies are described in the “Website Analytics, Digital Advertising & Cookies” section below and are subject to the consent preferences you set there. Our form processing is provided by Web3Forms, which handles submissions under its own privacy terms.

Website Analytics, Digital Advertising & Cookies

Added November 2026.

Website analytics and performance measurement

Trillas Consulting Engineers uses website analytics and measurement technologies, including Google Analytics 4 (GA4), to understand website traffic, evaluate visitor interactions, and improve the performance and functionality of our website.

These technologies may collect information such as pages visited, referral sources, approximate geographic location, device and browser information, and website engagement activity. We use this information to evaluate website performance, improve our content and services, and understand how visitors discover and interact with our website.

Digital advertising and conversion tracking

Trillas Consulting Engineers uses digital advertising platforms, including Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram), and potentially other advertising networks, to promote our engineering services and reach relevant professional audiences.

We may use tracking technologies provided by these platforms to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and understand visitor actions after interacting with our advertisements. These actions may include visiting specific website pages, submitting inquiry forms, clicking telephone links, or contacting our company.

Depending on the platform and technologies implemented, information collected may include advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device and browser information, referral sources, website interactions, and conversion events. We use this information to measure advertising performance, improve campaign targeting, and evaluate the effectiveness of our marketing activities.

Enhanced conversion measurement

Certain advertising platforms, including Google Ads, may offer enhanced conversion measurement features that improve the accuracy of advertising attribution. When permitted, limited first-party information voluntarily provided through website forms, such as email addresses or telephone numbers, may be securely hashed and transmitted to participating advertising platforms for conversion matching. Hashing transforms information into an encoded representation but does not necessarily make it anonymous.

We use these technologies for advertising measurement in accordance with applicable privacy requirements, platform policies, and visitor consent preferences.

Phone call conversion tracking

We may use advertising platform call reporting and conversion tracking technologies to measure telephone inquiries generated through digital advertising campaigns. Depending on the platform, these technologies may measure call activity, such as call initiation, timing, duration, and conversion status. Where supported, advertising platforms may use forwarding numbers to attribute telephone inquiries to specific advertisements.

We use this information to evaluate advertising performance and improve our marketing efforts. Call tracking does not necessarily involve recording telephone conversations. If call recording is introduced, additional disclosures and consent requirements may apply.

Remarketing and advertising personalization

We may use remarketing and audience-building technologies offered by Google, LinkedIn, Meta, and other advertising platforms to display relevant advertisements to individuals who have previously visited our website or interacted with our digital content. These technologies may use cookies, pixels, advertising identifiers, and information about website interactions to support audience creation, advertising measurement, and personalized advertising.

Where required by applicable law, these activities are subject to visitor consent or other applicable privacy rights and choices. Visitors may manage certain advertising preferences directly through the privacy and advertising settings offered by the respective platforms.

Cookies, pixels, and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies, tracking pixels, tags, and similar technologies for website functionality, analytics, advertising measurement, and remarketing. These technologies may be provided by third-party services, including Google, LinkedIn, Meta, and other analytics or advertising providers.

Cookies and similar technologies may collect or process information about browser activity, device characteristics, website interactions, and advertising engagement. We use these technologies to support website operations, understand visitor behavior, measure marketing performance, and improve the relevance of our advertising.

Cookie consent and preference management

Our website provides a consent management mechanism that allows visitors to review and manage preferences for non-essential cookies and tracking technologies. Where required by applicable law, visitors may accept or reject optional analytics and advertising technologies and update their preferences through the website’s cookie settings.

We use Google Consent Mode v2 to communicate applicable visitor consent choices to Google services, including Google Analytics and Google Ads. Consent choices for other advertising platforms, such as LinkedIn and Meta, are managed through the website’s consent management implementation and the applicable platform integrations. Depending on the technical configuration and applicable legal requirements, certain limited measurement signals may be processed without storing advertising or analytics cookies.

Visitors may also manage cookies through their browser settings.

Third-party privacy information

For additional information about how third-party platforms process information, visitors may review their respective privacy policies: Google · LinkedIn · Meta. These third-party platforms maintain their own privacy practices, data processing terms, and advertising preference controls.
