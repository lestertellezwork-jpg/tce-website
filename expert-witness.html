05 For Counsel

Answers, up front.

When should an attorney retain a forensic engineering expert?
Early enough to evaluate relevant physical evidence before conditions change, repairs occur, or potentially important evidence is altered or lost.
Does the engineer who inspects also testify?
Yes. The engineer responsible for the opinions remains personally involved from site inspection through analysis, reports, deposition and trial — preserving continuity between what was observed and how it is explained.
In which jurisdictions can TCE serve as an engineering expert?
TCE is licensed in 30 states, D.C. and Puerto Rico — 32 jurisdictions — and accepts expert engagements nationwide.
